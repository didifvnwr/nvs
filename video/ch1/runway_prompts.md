# Runway — פרומפטים לדרפט 15 שניות (3 שוטים × 5 שניות)

העבודה: Image-to-Video. מעלים את תמונת ההתחלה (video/out/runway_refs/) וכותבים את הפרומפט באנגלית.
סגנון קבוע לכל השוטים: stylized 3D animated short film, soft clay-like characters, warm dawn light, pastel colors, gentle depth of field, smooth cinematic camera.

## שוט 1 (0–5 שניות) — תמונה: shot1_start_exterior.png
Smooth floating camera glides forward and slightly down over a small town at sunrise toward one cozy house, then flies through its open window into a warm bedroom. Soft golden light, floating dust in the sunbeam. Stylized 3D animated short film, soft clay-like look, warm dawn light, pastel colors, smooth cinematic camera.

## שוט 2 (5–10 שניות) — תמונה: shot2_start_bedroom.png
Slow floating camera arcs around the bed. A man on the left and a woman on the right sleep peacefully in identical poses. A red alarm clock on the nightstand rings and shakes. The man opens his eyes and sits up first. Stylized 3D animated short film, soft clay-like characters, warm morning light, pastel colors.

## שוט 3 (10–15 שניות) — תמונה: shot3_start_sitting.png
The man swings his legs off the bed and plants his feet on the worn spot of the rug, stands up, stretches his arms with a small relaxed smile, then walks toward the bright bathroom doorway. The camera slowly pulls back and follows. His partner keeps sleeping. Stylized 3D animated short film, soft clay-like characters, warm light, pastel colors.
